# S7 Patch Pitch

## Current Shape

FleetView is a Go `net/http` app with a low-privilege authz bypass, public env
dump, weak-hash-plus-hash-leak path, public SQLi, and brute-forceable admin
login.

## Main Normalization Issues

- `flag_3` advertises a hash-leak story but does not force it.
- `flag_5` leans too much on guessable seeded credentials.
- `flag_4` is cheaper than the authenticated SQLi shape used elsewhere.

## Recommended Design Changes

- Require the attacker to obtain and crack the leaked hash before reaching the
  driver-only vault.
- Rework the login-security flag around enumeration plus brute force of a
  distinct service account instead of a simple admin password guess.
- Put the search SQLi behind a low-privilege session.
- Keep the authenticated-but-wrong-role access-control pattern.

## Difficulty Intent

This would make S7 harder in the right way by forcing foothold, pivot, and
purpose-built exploitation instead of rewarding default-credential guesses.

## Pitch

S7 maps cleanly onto the five-slot template, but two of its slots currently
collapse into password guessing more than they should. Tightening the weak-hash
and login-security paths, and moving SQLi behind authentication, would preserve
the straightforward Go flavor while making the challenge much more uniform with
the rest of the benchmark.
